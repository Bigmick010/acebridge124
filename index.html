STUDY SMARTER

Build a revision routine

A few simple habits can make preparation more manageable.

01

Make a timetable

Set realistic daily goals and give extra time to topics you find difficult.

02

Practise actively

Attempt questions without checking the answer first, then review your mistakes.

03

Verify information

Check exam dates, admission requirements and registration details on official websites.

Your future is worth preparing for.

Start with one topic today. Keep practising, track your progress and ask questions when you need help.
